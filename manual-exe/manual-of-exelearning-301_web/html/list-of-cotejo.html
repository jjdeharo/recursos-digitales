List of Cotejo

What is it?

With this iDevice we can to introduce a list of quotes to provide a structured and easy to understand list of the activities proposed throughout the educational resourceIt will be a useful instrument for the self-regulation of the students’ learning, allowing them to carry out the follow-up of the activities they are completing.

We will be able to configure the list of quotation with paragraphs and subparagraphs so that the student mark the activities carried out, select the state of the activity from a displaysable or enter a text content.

Examples of use

Complete the list by marking the boxes when performing the corresponding activities.

General Adjustments

In the "Options" section we will enter the Title and Subtitle of the rating list. We will be able to configure different options: display user data, remember marked options, assign scores, include logo or decorative images... We can configurate our rating list including activities of a different type:

  • The BoxA verification box will appear for students to mark it when they have done the specified activity.
  • The textA verification box will appear for students to mark it when they have done the specified activity and a text box to introduce content.
  • SelectedThe student must mark in a dischargeable if the activity is done, in process or without completion.
  • Not to mark: Only a text is presented, without the possibility of marking or entering content. This option is the recommended for titles of paragraphs or subparagraphs, for example.
