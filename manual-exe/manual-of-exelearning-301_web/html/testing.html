How is it done?

When selecting the iDevice "Test" from the list of iDivices, the following will be shown in our eXeLearning:

In the top, we will have the possibility to modify the title and assign an icon, once the iDevice is saved. Last, we’ll have the option to include a subsequent content. We see that there are a number of options and tabs, each with a different functionality that will allow us to configure our game in a very flexible way 

General Adjustments


The tab "General Adjustments" is the one that is displayed by default when creating the iDevice "Test".

In this tab we will define the activities proposed on the iDevice, instructions to perform the game, the different configurations and options and the questions we will make to our students.

  • By clicking on the "Instruktions" link, a text box will be opened where we will be able to specify the instructions to follow.
  • By clicking on the "Options" link we will be able to configure from the way to display the iDevice, the number of lives, the score system, if we want to show the random questions and the answers to such questions as well the solutions to them after being answered. We will be able to include an introductory video at the beginning of the activity and the option to mark the Progress Report with its corresponding identifier.
  • In the "Questions" link we will have the option to create our questions accompanied by support multimedia, being able to choose up to four possible response options (only one will be valid) and the maximum time to answer. No Multimedia, image, The video and The text.

    In the case of introducing a VideoThe steps must be:

    1. Enter the URL of the video to enter
    2. Click on the Play button for the video to start playing (the video time will be displayed on the gray button)
    3. You can only play a fragment of the video. for this we will do the following:
      1. To indicate the start time, we will stop the video at the exact point we want to start (the time will appear on the gray button). Then we will click on "Start" (it will be marked with a blue edge) and then we will press on the grey time button. 
      2. To indicate the end time, we will stop the video at the exact point we want to finish (the time will appear on the gray button). Then we will click on "Start" (it will be marked with a blue edge) and then we will press on the grey time button. 
    4. We can also silence a part of the video, indicating the start time and duration.

Use of the icons  We will be able to create new questions, access the questions already made to edit them in addition to deleting, copying and cutting questions.

Contrasigns

In the "Contraseñas" tab, we will be able to create a challenge itinerary in which players will not be allowed to access a new game or challenge until they get a key in a previous activity. For this, we will be able to set up an access code as well as a message that will be shown to players when they reach a fixed percentage of accidents, and that they will be allowed to use as a password for a new challenge or a subsequent activity.

Both are optional, and your configuration will only appear if we mark them, as in the image.

Scorry

In the "SCORM" tab we will be able to determine if we want to save the results obtained by our students and in what conditions we want it to do when we export our contents.

It should be noted that this option of storage It will only be available for SCORM exports and when we publish our content on platforms such as: by Moodle or other LMS platforms compatible with SCORM.

Table where we will be able to customize the automatic texts and messages that the iDevice generates.

Import and Export

In the “Import/Export” tab we can imported Questions compatible with this activity from txt or xml files (Moodle), or we can Exporting Questions in txt format to integrate them into other compatible activities.

IA

This tab allows you to bring questions from an AI chatbot in a simple way. So when opening it is shown to us, as an example a Prompt (model) that we can take to a chatbot that will generate questions in the appropriate format to be paste on the "Questions" tab. These questions proposed by AI will be added to the iDevice when we save ready to be used in the activity.
