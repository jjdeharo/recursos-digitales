Gallery of Images

What is it?

We will use this device for Showing an album of images, initially in miniature formatBy clicking on any of the images of the album, we will open an extended sample, with possibility of navigation between the different images in the gallery.

How is it done?

 When selecting the iDevice "Gallery of Images" from the list of iDivices, the following will be shown in our eXeLearning:

Add images from the button Adding images.

Once the first is added, you will be able to continue adding (or dragging from a folder), deleting and moving from the buttons below each of them.
