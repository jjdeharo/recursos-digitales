The FX Effects

What is it?

The FX button allows us to create multiple effects on our contents to structure the information and prevent that there is a lot of scrolling down.

by Acordeon

The "Acordeon" effect has the content within a list that can be displayed and collapsed by clicking on each title.

Spring

From 21 March to 20 June, spring marks the beginning of a more temperate climate after winter. During these months, temperatures rise gradually, days are prolonged and vegetation begins to develop rapidly. It is a stage in which the presence of pollen increases, which influences both agriculture and the health of allergic people.

Summer

Between 21 June and 22 September, summer is characterized by being the most hot season of the year in the northern hemisphere. Days are longer than nights, which generates greater exposure to the sun. It is a season linked to rest, holidays and outdoor activities, although it also involves risks such as drought or heat waves in some regions.

Autumn

From 23 September to 20 December, autumn presents a gradual decline in temperature and a reduction in daily light hours. It is common that falling leaf trees lose their sheet, leaving characteristic landscapes of brown shades and ocres. In many countries, this season also marks the start of harvests and winter preparation.

Winter

From December 21 to March 20, the winter is the coldest season of the year, with short days and long nights. In much of the northern hemisphere ice and snow are recorded, which influences transportation, agriculture and everyday life. It is a stage where heating use increases and is associated with important festivities such as Christmas and New Year.

How is it done?

Creating an acordeon type effect is very simple. for this we will follow the following steps:

1. We click on the buttonof the editor.

2. In the window that is displayed, we select the desired type of effect (Acordeon, Tables, Pagation or Carrusel)

3. You will create a box with two headlines (which we can edit to put the title we want) and two spaces to write content to show or collapse.We will be able to edit it and incorporate any kind of multimedia resource.

4. To create a new item from the list, we will write the title we want to incorporate and the desired content within the created FX box. We will mark the title and select the "Head 2" format option.

5. To change the effect, you just have to click on the FX button again and select another.
