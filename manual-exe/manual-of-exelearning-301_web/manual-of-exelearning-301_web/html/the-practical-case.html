Manual of eXeLearning 3.0.1

The practical case

What is it?

The iDevice "Practical Case" is an adaptation of the iDEVice "Text" with a box, without feedback for the "History"or the narrative to work this task and another or other boxes, these with feedback to establish the "Activitiesof the practical case.

Examples of use

A few months ago, it was announced what is considered by some "the greatest scientific discovery in history": the demonstration of the existence of the so-called Higgs Boson.

From this presentation, we propose you to carry out your own research on this topic from some activities.

Search for information:

We ask you to look for two or three internet pages where you can find information about the Higgs Boson: who was its discoverer, what theory he presented and where and how that theory has finally been demonstrated.

It is very important that you make sure that the sources of information you have found are appropriate and that you will be able to access them for a certain time, i.e. that they are stable pages.

The oral exhibition:

From the information obtained in the sources you have consulted before, you have to prepare an oral exhibition in which you explain the essential aspects about the topic we are working on.

The exhibition may not last more than five minutes. It is mandatory that you use some support resource. It may be a presentation that you have created or some material like that we have shown you to appear on the Internet.

How is it done?

When selecting the iDevice "Practical Case" from the list of iDivices, the following will be shown in our eXeLearning:

In the top, we will have the possibility to modify the title and assign an icon, once the iDevice is saved. We see that there are a series of options each with a different functionality that will allow us to configure our activity in a very flexible way.

Information of the task

As a text iDevice, it allows to assign time and groups to the task that the student will perform.

History

In this field we will write the narrative of the activity with what is necessary to carry it out, a video, a reading...

Activities

Here we indicate the instructions to carry out the task that is considered, allowing "Most feedback" that serves as a help or complement to the student.
