The Challenge

What is it?

We will use this device for Create games of the type "Remove Room" (The Escape RoomIn which players will have to solve different challenges before solving the final challenge.

Examples of use

Resolve all the challenges and complete the challenge.

1

The combination of the secret box consists of 4 numbers. the first two you will get them in the first challenge, and the last two in the second.

Once you’ve achieved them, write both numbers consistently and you’ll be able to finish the challenge.

How is it done?

When selecting the iDevice "Development" from the list of iDivices, the following will be shown in our eXeLearning:

On the top, we will have the possibility to modify the title and assign an icon, once the iDevice is saved. We see that there are a series of options and tabs, each with a different functionality that will allow us to configure our game in a very flexible way 

General Adjustments

The "General Adjustments" tab is the one that is displayed by default when creating the iDevice Challenge.

In this tab we will define the activities proposed in the iDevice, instructions to perform the game, the different configurations and options and the definitions of the words or expressions that our students should know.

  • By clicking on the "Instruktions" section, a text box will be opened where we will be able to specify the instructions to follow.
  • By clicking on the "Options" section we will be able to define whether we want to minimize the iDevice and the option to mark the Progress Report with your corresponding ID.

  • In the Questions section we have two tables: The Challengein which the challenge to which the players will face will be explained and the time they have to introduce the final response, and Challenges, where they will go defining the different intermediate challenges that players must continue to overcome. In these challenges you can define up to 3 pistes that will go appearing past the time defined for each of them.


personalized texts

Table where we will be able to customize the automatic texts and messages that the iDevice generates.

Scorry

In the "SCORM" tab we will be able to determine if we want to save the results obtained by our students and in what conditions we want it to do when we export our contents.

It should be noted that this option of storage It will only be available for SCORM exports and when we publish our content on platforms such as: by Moodle or other LMS platforms compatible with SCORM.
