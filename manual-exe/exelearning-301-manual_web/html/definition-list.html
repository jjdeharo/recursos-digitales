eXeLearning 3.0.1 Manual

Definition list

What is it?

The "Definition List" allows us to create a list of terms or concepts in which their definition or explanation is hidden. We can view each definition or explanation by clicking on the term.

OER

Open Educational Resources (OER) are learning, teaching, and research materials in any format and medium that are in the public domain or are under copyright and have been released under an open license, which permits no-cost access, reuse, repurposing, adaptation, and redistribution by others.


An open license refers to a license that respects the intellectual property rights of the copyright owner and provides permissions granting the public the rights to access, reuse, repurpose, adapt, and redistribute educational materials.

Free Software

Free software (in English free software, although this term is sometimes confused with "gratis" due to the ambiguity of the term free in the English language, so libre software is also used) is the designation for software that respects the freedom of all users who acquired the product and, therefore, once obtained, it can be used, copied, studied, modified, and redistributed freely in various ways.

According to its main promoter, the Free Software Foundation, free software refers to the users' freedom to run, copy, distribute, and study the software, and even modify it and distribute modified versions. (from Wikipedia)

Commons

The Space Commons began its journey in April 2014 with the purpose of becoming a meeting place for the exchange of impressions and proposals about educational experiences and learning among teachers. At the same time, it was presented as a reference site for accessing the open educational digital resources (OER) repository of the Ministry of Education, Vocational Training and Sports and of the Autonomous Communities.

How is it done?

To create a definition list we must follow the following steps:

  • We click on the icon  and the first Term with its Definition appears
  • We replace Term with the concept or term we want to define.
  • We replace Definition of the term with the description or definition of the term. 
  • To add a new term, we just need to position ourselves at the end of the previous definition and click again on the "Definition list" icon
