eXeLearning 3.0.1 Manual

What is eXeLearning

eXeLearning is a free, open-source software program (open source) designed for the creation and editing of interactive digital resources. It is a tool that allows the incorporation of a wide variety of content - texts, images, videos, interactive activities or games - in a simple and structured way, offering a clean and attractive appearance.


Cedec. eXeLearning 3.0 (CC BY-SA)



The tool is available in two modes:

  • Installable version: to work locally
  • Online version: facilitates quick editing and publishing of content on the platform where it is integrated.

Resources created with eXeLearning have a responsivedesign, ensuring their correct display on any device, and can be exported in standard formats such as HTML, SCORM or EPUB3, which promotes their dissemination on platforms and repositories.

Furthermore, eXeLearning encourages the creation of Open Educational Resources (OER), promoting shared knowledge and open education.

The origin of eXeLearning dates back to the 2000s, within the framework of the project eXeLearning.org developed in New Zealand. In 2010, the INTEF (then ITE) took over the development and promoted eXeLearning.net as an evolution of the original project, delegating coordination to Cedec (an organization dependent on INTEF and the Junta de Extremadura). Since then, it has consolidated as a collaborative project involving users, public administrations, and other entities.


Over the years, the tool has experienced significant advances, incorporating improvements and functionalities and increasing the user community. An online version has been developed, facilitating its integration with Moodle, and other developments have been made such as the eXeReader viewer.

Version 3.0 represents a great leap in the evolution of the tool. It involves a complete renewal of the code and the interface and incorporates major usability and functionality improvements (such as collaborative work in its online version).

This development and its continuous improvements are the result of close collaboration between the MEFPD and the regional educational administrations of Spain. 

Collaborative project

The progress of eXeLearning is the result of close collaboration between the Ministry of Education, Vocational Training and Sports of Spain (MEFPD) —through Cedec-INTEF— and various regional educational administrations, including Extremadura, Canarias, Comunidad Valenciana, Galicia, Madrid, and Andalucía.

The vitality of the project is also due to the active participation of its community, which contributes with contributions, bug reports, and suggestions. Furthermore, it offers support on channels such as Telegram, where doubts are resolved and knowledge is shared openly.

The development of eXeLearning is managed completely openly on GitHub, which acts as the nerve center of the project. There, developers can:

  • Set up a local environment in minutes using Docker commands (docker run, docker exec) or clone the repository with git clone and use make up.
  • Consult the complete documentation in the directory doc/.
  • Use the Makefile to simplify tasks like starting/stopping containers, running tests, or updating translations.
  • Fork the project, create branches, make changes, and submit Pull Requests.

The community on GitHub is always available to answer questions and receive bug reports in the issuessection. This open management reinforces the collaborative and constantly improving nature of eXeLearning.

You can learn more about the project in the section The eXeLearning Project on the official website.
