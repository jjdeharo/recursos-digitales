How is it done?

When selecting the "VideoTest" iDevice from the list of iDevices, the following will be displayed in our eXeLearning:

At the top, we will have the option to modify the title and assign an icon, once the iDevice is saved. Finally, we will have the option to include subsequent content. We see that there are a series of options and tabs, each with a different functionality that will allow us to configure our game in a very flexible way 

General Settings


The "General Settings" tab is the one displayed by default when creating the VideoTest iDevice.

In this tab we will define the activities proposed in the iDevice, instructions for carrying out the game, the different configurations and options, and the questions associated with the video that our students will watch.

  • By clicking on the "Instructions" link, a text box will open where we can state the instructions to follow.
  • By clicking on the "Options" link, we can configure whether we want to show the solutions randomly and the time when the solution will be displayed, the scoring system, the number of lives, whether we want to minimize the iDevice, or the option to mark the Progress Report with its corresponding identifier.
  • In the Questions link, we will have the option to enter a YouTube video or a file, write the questions, and decide at which second they will appear to our students. The steps should be:
    1. Enter the URL of the video to be inserted.
    2. Click the Play button for the video to start playing (the video time will be displayed on the gray button located below the viewer)
    3. Be able to play only a fragment of the video. To do this, we will do the following:
      1. To indicate the start time, we will stop the video at the exact point where we want to start (the time will appear on the gray button). Then, we will click "Start" (it will be marked with a blue border) and then press the gray time button. 
      2. To indicate the end time, we will stop the video at the exact point where we want to finish (the time will appear on the gray button). Then, we will click "Start" (it will be marked with a blue border) and then press the gray time button. 
    4. To enter a question at a specific moment, we will proceed in the same way, selecting in this case the "Question Point" field.
    5. We can also mute a part of the video, indicating the start time and the duration.
    Using the icons  we will be able to navigate through our questions, add new ones, or delete them.

Passwords

In the "Passwords" tab, we can create a challenge itinerary in which players cannot access a new game or challenge until they obtain a key in a previous activity. For this, we can set an access code as well as a message that will be shown to players when they reach a fixed percentage of correct answers, and which they can use as a password for a new challenge or a subsequent activity.

Both are optional, and their configuration will only appear if we mark them, as in the image.

SCORM

In the "SCORM" tab, we can determine whether we want to save the results obtained by our students and under what conditions we want to do so when we export our content.

It should be noted that this save option will only be available for SCORM exports and when we publish our content on platforms such as Moodle, eScholarium or SCORM-compatible LMS platforms.
