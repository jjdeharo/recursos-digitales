eXeLearning 3.0.1 Manual

Practical case

What is it?

The "Case Study" iDevice is an adaptation of the "Text" iDevice with one box, without feedback for the "Story", or the narrative to work on this task and another box or boxes, these with feedback to establish the "Activities" of the Case Study.

Usage example

A few months ago, what is considered by some as "the greatest scientific discovery in history" was announced: the demonstration of the existence of the so-called Higgs Boson. But what exactly is the Higgs Boson? Why is this discovery so important?

Based on this presentation, we propose that you carry out your own research on this topic through some activities.

Information search:

We ask you to search for two or three Internet pages where you can find information about the Higgs Boson: who its discoverer was, what the theory presented is, and where and how that theory has finally been demonstrated.

It is very important that you ensure the information sources you have found are suitable and that you will be able to access them for a certain period of time, meaning they are stable pages.

Oral presentation:

Based on the information obtained from the sources you consulted earlier, you must prepare an oral presentation in which you explain the essential aspects of the topic we are working on. You can also present the information sources you have used.

The presentation cannot last more than five minutes. It is mandatory to use some support resource. It can be a presentation you have created or some material like the one we have shown you that appears on the Internet.

How is it done?

When selecting the "Practical Case" iDevice from the list of iDevices, the following will be displayed in our eXeLearning:

At the top, we will have the possibility to modify the title and assign an icon, once the iDevice is saved. We see that there are a series of options, each with a different functionality that will allow us to configure our activity in a very flexible way.

Task information

As a text iDevice allows assigning time and groupings to the task that the students will perform.

Story

In this field, we will write the narrative of the activity with what is necessary to carry it out, a video, a reading...

Activities

Here we indicate the instructions to carry out the considered task, allowing "Show feedback" that serves as help or complement to the students. We can add several "Activities" with the same structure
