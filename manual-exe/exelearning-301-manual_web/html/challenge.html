eXeLearning 3.0.1 Manual

Challenge

What is it?

We will use this iDevice to create "escape room" type games (escape room) in which players must solve different challenges before solving the final challenge.

Usage example

Solve all the challenges and complete the challenge.

1

The combination of the secret box consists of 4 numbers. You will get the first two in the first challenge, and the last two in the second.

Once you have obtained them, write both numbers consecutively and you will be able to finish the challenge. 

You have 10 minutes!

Solve: 2x3+4x3+3

Solve: 200/2-50/5-40

How is it done?

When selecting the "Challenge" iDevice from the list of iDevices, the following will be displayed in our eXeLearning:

At the top, we will have the possibility to modify the title and assign an icon, once the iDevice is saved. We see that there are a series of options and tabs, each with a different functionality that will allow us to configure our game in a very flexible way. 

General Settings

The "General Settings" tab is the one displayed by default when creating the Challenge iDevice.

In this tab we will define the activities proposed in the iDevice, instructions for carrying out the game, the different configurations and options, and the definitions of the words or expressions that our students should know.

  • When clicking on the "Instructions" section, a text box will open where we can state the instructions to follow.
  • By clicking on the "Options" section, we can define whether we want to minimize the iDevice and the option to mark the Progress Report with its corresponding identifier.

  • In the Questions section we have two tabs: Challenge, in which the challenge that the players will face and the time they have to enter the final answer will be explained, and Challenges, where the different intermediate challenges that the players must continue to overcome will be defined. In these challenges, up to 3 clues can be defined, which will appear after the time defined for each of them has passed.


Custom texts

Tab where we can customize the texts and automatic messages generated by the iDevice.

SCORM

In the "SCORM" tab, we can determine whether we want to save the results obtained by our students and under what conditions we want to do so when we export our content.

It should be noted that this save option will only be available for SCORM exports and when we publish our content on platforms such as Moodle or other SCORM-compatible LMS platforms.
