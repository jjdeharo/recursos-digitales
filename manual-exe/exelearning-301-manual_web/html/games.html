eXeLearning 3.0.1 Manual

Games

These resources are designed to integrate playful mechanics into learning, with the aim of motivating users and reinforcing knowledge without the direct pressure of a formal assessment.

Within this specific category, the available iDevices include:

  • Word search
  • Lock
  • Crossword
  • Challenge
  • Discover (2,3,4)
  • Hidden image
  • Puzzle
  • Wheel
  • TriviExt
