eXeLearning 3.0.1 Manual

Work elements

Next, we will explore two essential elements in creating resources with eXeLearning: iDevices, which allow incorporating and structuring interactive content, and the text editor, a fundamental tool for shaping and formatting the presented information, included in a large part of the iDevices.


TEXT EDITOR

However, before looking at these iDevices in detail, let's pause to analyze the functionalities included in the Text editor, another fundamental working element that is present in many iDevices and that allows introducing and formatting textual content, including multimedia elements, structuring text, incorporating mathematical or musical notation, or recording audio. It is therefore fundamental and transversal in resource creation. We will see this editor in detail, showing its basic importance.


iDEVICES

The main element for incorporating content into resources created with eXeLearning is the "iDevice" or content block. These iDevices allow enriching resources and bringing them to life, enabling the incorporation of a wide variety of materials, from texts, images, and videos, to digital objects, interactive activities, or games. eXeLearning 3. 0 has integrated numerous new iDevices, which are conveniently organized into categories to optimize the content design process:

  • Information and presentation: To display and structure content, enriching it with accessibility and various media.
  • Interactive activities: Exercises that require direct interaction for active learning.
  • Games: Resources that use playful mechanics to motivate and reinforce learning without evaluative pressure.
  • Assessment and tracking: Tools to verify knowledge and progress, with the possibility of offering feedback.
  • Sciences: Specifically designed to work with content from specific subjects or areas
